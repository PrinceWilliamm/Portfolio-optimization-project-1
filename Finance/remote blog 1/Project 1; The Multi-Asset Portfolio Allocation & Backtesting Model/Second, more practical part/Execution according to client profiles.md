# Example client profiles
### 1. Aggressive investor
Has a rather rigorous profit target and wants the portfolio to have a lower lowatility than SP500. Not possible to some extent but with small adjustions to the assets, the clients needs are met. The assets maintain for exaple 75% of the desired profits with for example 50% of the volatility the SP500 has. 
### 2. Low volatility, beats savings account dude
Cares about his money, wants the portoflio to be performing even during the hardest economical situations, doesn't care so much about the rigorous profits. Only goal is to have better profits than the average savings account but not by high volatility. Seems easy, right. Not.
