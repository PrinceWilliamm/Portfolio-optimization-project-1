## Macroeconomic events ##
- NFP
	- Nonfarm Payrolls = number of workers in US without taking to account the people who work on farms, private households, nonprofits or employ themselves. Examples on EUR/USD; Lower unemployment, downtrend, if higher unemployment uptrend. Roughly
- CPI
	- Consumer Price Index = Compares around 80 000 products and their prices, making a large variety of different sectors and their products prices. If prices rise = inflation = EUR/USD up. If prices dip = deflation = EUR/USD down. 
- FOMC
	- The central bank of USA and its press conferences are one of the major things that have an effect on the EUR/USD. They decide on interest rates and economic policies.
