### 2. Sequences, Series, and Binomials

|**Notation**|**Meaning / Read As**|**Example / Context**|
|---|---|---|
|$u_n$|The $n$-th term of a sequence|$u_1$ is the first term|
|$d$|Common difference|In Arithmetic Progressions (AP): $u_n = u_1 + (n-1)d$|
|$r$|Common ratio|In Geometric Progressions (GP): $u_n = u_1 r^{n-1}$|
|$S_n$|Sum of the first $n$ terms|$S_n = u_1 + u_2 + \dots + u_n$|
|$S_\infty$|Sum to infinity|Geometric series where $\vert{}r\vert{} < 1$|
|$\sum_{i=1}^n u_i$|Sigma notation (summation)|$u_1 + u_2 + \dots + u_n$|
|$n!$|$n$ factorial|$5! = 5 \times 4 \times 3 \times 2 \times 1 = 120$|
|$\binom{n}{r}$ or $^n\mathrm{C}_r$|Binomial coefficient ($n$ choose $r$)|$\frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}$|

## Basic Theory
Arithmetic sequences are often expressed with the following formulas depending on the topic:
	*n*th term = $$u_n = u_1 + (n - 1)d$$
	Sum of first *n* terms = $$S_n = \frac{n}{2}\left(2u_1 + (n - 1)d\right)$$
	Sigma notation = $$\sum_{k=1}^{n} (3k + 2)$$
