## Section A: Core Approaches

- **Structure:** 2 compulsory short-answer questions (SAQs).
- **Marks:** 4 marks per question (total of 8 marks).
- **Content:** Covers the core content areas—the biological, cognitive, or sociocultural approaches.
- **Focus:** Tests direct knowledge, conceptual understanding, and explanation of approach-specific theories. Research studies are optional.
- **Length/Time:** Roughly 200–250 words or 10 minutes per answer.
## Section B: Application to Unseen Situations

- **Structure:** 2 compulsory short-answer questions (ASAQs) applying content to scenarios.
- **Marks:** 6 marks per question (total of 12 marks).
- **Content:** Draws from four applied contexts: _Health and wellbeing_, _Human development_, _Human relationships_, or _Learning and cognition_.
- **Focus:** Tests application (AO2) rather than rote memorization. You must use the specific unseen scenario/context provided in the exam prompt. Research studies are generally not required here.
- **Length/Time:** Roughly 350–450 words or 15–20 minutes per answer.
