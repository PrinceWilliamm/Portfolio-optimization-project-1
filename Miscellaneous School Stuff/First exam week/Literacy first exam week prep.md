
# PAP 1
## Main structure
- Introduction
- Body paragraphs (3-5)
- Conclusion
### Introduction in more detail
In couple of senteces exaplain the author, text type, what is it about etc. Also make a thesis statement and a summary statement.
Example of an introductory text:
	In this {insert text type}, {insert author} discusses the {insert you chosen theme or point of view} through {insert Criteria B words about your analysis}. {thesis statement formatted nicely and embedded cleverly}.
### Body paragraphs in a bit more detail
Using the PEEL technique which states the next
P = Point
E = Explore
E = Evidence
L = Link to the thesis
	Go through a couple of the chosen theme by using the Criteria B words and use a paragraph for each larger topic such as symbolism, structure, authorial choices, characters etc.
### Conclusion in a bit less detail than others
Conclusion is the first thing to cut off if everything fails --> It is not so important
	IF and only if you make one, make it well
		Do not add new stuff, do not stirr the pot anymore
Example Conclusion
	Through all of these things, {author} handles/discusses/addresses etc. the {thesis statement} thoroughly. As all of the variables are noted the work reveals itself to be a work of art.
