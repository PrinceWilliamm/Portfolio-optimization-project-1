# Simple Pendulum Theory

## What is the concept?

A simple pendulum is a mass hanging from a fixed point by a string or rod. When it is pulled away from its resting position and released, gravity pulls it back toward equilibrium and it swings back and forth.

## Why does it matter for the project?

The simulation needs a clear physical model. The simple pendulum is a good first model because it connects physics, mathematics, programming, graphs, and experimental comparison.

## Key idea

For small angles, an ideal pendulum behaves approximately like simple harmonic motion.
