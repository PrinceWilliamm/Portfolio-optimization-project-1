# Small-Angle Approximation

## What is the concept?

For small angles, the pendulum equations become much simpler. This is called the small-angle approximation.

## Why does it matter for the project?

The basic period formula works best when the starting angle is small. If the angle is large, the ideal formula becomes less accurate.

## First-version decision

Use a small initial angle first, such as 5 degrees or 10 degrees, so the simulation can be compared with the theoretical period formula.

## Later comparison

After the minimum viable project is complete, the group can compare angles such as:

- 5 degrees
- 10 degrees
- 20 degrees
- 45 degrees
- 70 degrees
