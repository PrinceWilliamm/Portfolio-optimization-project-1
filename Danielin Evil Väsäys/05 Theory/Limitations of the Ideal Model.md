# Limitations of the Ideal Model

## What is limited?

The ideal model is useful, but it is not the same as a real pendulum.

## Main limitations

- Real strings have mass.
- Real pendulum bobs are not perfect point masses.
- Air resistance exists.
- Pivot friction exists.
- Large angles behave differently from the small-angle approximation.
- Real measurements can have timing and setup errors.

## Why does it matter for the project?

The final report should explain that the model is intentionally simplified. The goal is not to perfectly copy reality, but to understand the basic relationship between length, gravity, and period.
