# Assumptions of the Ideal Model

## What is the concept?

An ideal model simplifies the real pendulum so the motion is easier to understand and calculate.

## Assumptions

- The string or rod is massless.
- The pendulum bob is treated as a point mass. (no physical dimensions only treated as a point in space.)
- The pendulum bob is treated as a point mass.
- There is no air resistance.
- There is no friction at the pivot.
- Gravity is constant.
- The motion happens in one vertical plane.
- The starting angle is small enough for the small-angle approximation to be useful.

## Why does it matter for the project?

These assumptions define what the first simulation is allowed to include. If a graph does not match real-world behavior perfectly, the assumptions help explain why.
