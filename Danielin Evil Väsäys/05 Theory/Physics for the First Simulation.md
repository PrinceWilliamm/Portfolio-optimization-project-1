

First simulation will be about the idealized case of the simple gravity pendulum. This note will go over all quantities and equations that are useful to calculate regarding the pendulum.

***Quantities***


$$
	T=period [s]
$$
The period of a pendulum is the time a pendulum takes to swing back to its starting position where it was first let go. It is a quantity that is measured in seconds.

$$
	f=\frac{1}{T}[Hz]
$$

Frequency is the inverse of the period. It has the units of Hz. 

$$
	l=length[m]
$$
Length of the rod from which an object is swinging from.


***Equations***

**1.**
First equation we are interested in relates the length of the rod, the period of the pendulum and the acceleration due to gravity. Note that this equation is an approximation.
$$
	T=2\pi \sqrt{ \frac{l}{g} }
$$
What can be seen from this equation is that the period is directly dictated by the length of the rod and the acceleration due to gravity. Note that the period does not depend on the mass of the object that is being swung. 

**2.**
Next equation is the equation of motion that describes the simple pendulum.
$$
	\theta(t)=A\cos (\omega t)+B\sin(\omega t)
$$
where A is the initial angle in radians and B times omega is the initial angular velocity
