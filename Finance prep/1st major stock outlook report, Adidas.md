## Overall view from 2026##
The 1st quarter was hard due to uncertanities in US market as well as some macroeconomic challenges. Still a huge upwards potential due to major celebrity collaborations and back-to-back record sales in sports sales with the FWC 26 finalists both being sponsored by adidas as well as the legendary sub 2 hour marathon ran by Sawe in the London marathon with adidas shoes. At the start of 3rd quarter, there was a earnings report that made the stock dump again. This was caused by international currency economics, retailer discounting and tariffs from US. Still, sales went up in all sectors and all geological locations by a lot and the possible US tariff refunds that are worth an estimated of 250 million werent even included in the report.

## Future outlook from a customer view ##
With these record sales taking over and more importantly the share buyback programme from Adidas themselves I feel that Adidas is gathering upwards potential for the October 9M report. This opinion is heavy biased as I've bought Adidas a week back in time. As funny as it sounds this 9M report seems to be heavily relied on geopolitical situations such as wars and inflation, things that have nothing to do with apparel sales. In more fundamental view the trend from adidas free time shoes as the campus or gazelles has gone away. This creates lower sales and gives more freedom for competitors like Nike but if taking into account that the sales of clothing has gone up more than shoes then I'd say that Adidas is doing just fine against for example Nike as prices rally in both lairs products.
