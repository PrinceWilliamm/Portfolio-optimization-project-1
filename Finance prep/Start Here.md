## Phase 1: High School (Years 2 & 3 / Grades 10–12)

**Academic & Skill Foundation**
- **Academic Focus:** Prioritize Microeconomics, Macroeconomics, Statistics, and Calculus.
    
- **Self-Study Foundations:** Complete free introductory modules on Corporate Finance Institute (CFI) or Khan Academy Finance.
    
- **Leadership:** Join or launch an Economics or Finance Club at school. Run stock pitch competitions or market news discussions.
    
**No-Code High School Projects**
- **Stock Coverage Reports:** Pick 2–3 companies you use daily (e.g., Apple, Nike, Spotify). Write 2-page equity research notes breaking down business models, revenue drivers, P/E ratios, and growth risks.
    
- **Weekly Market Brief Substack:** Write a 300-word weekly newsletter tracking central bank policy, major market moves, and notable corporate news.
    
- **VC Startup Sourcing Teardowns:** Select 3 early-stage startups per month. Write a 1-page investment memo covering:  
    - Problem & Solution
        
    - Total Addressable Market (TAM)
        
    - Competitor Moat
        
    - Investment Thesis (Why a fund should back them)
**Networking & Social Visibility**
- **LinkedIn Profile Setup:** Create a clean profile with a clear headline (e.g., _"High School Student | Aspiring Finance & Venture Analyst"_). Share PDF slides of your equity research notes.
    
- **Instagram Educational Account:** Post 5-slide carousels explaining finance mechanics simply (e.g., _"What is an LBO explained in 60 seconds"_ or _"How VCs Value a Pre-Revenue Startup"_).
    
- **Cold Outreach:** Contact alumni or local boutique wealth advisors for 15-minute informational interviews to ask about their daily routines.
## Phase 2: University (Years 1–4)
**Year 1: On-Campus Traction & Fundamentals**
- **Target Institutions:** Aim for target or semi-target university business/economics programs.
    
- **Student Investment Fund (SIF):** Join student-run portfolio funds or VC clubs. Aim for pitch leader or sector head roles by Year 2.
    
- **Technical Mastery:** Learn 3-statement financial modeling, DCF valuation, and Excel shortcuts.
    
**Year 2: Insight Schemes & Boutique Internships**
- **Spring Insight Programs:** Apply early in Autumn for bulge bracket and boutique investment bank insight schemes.
    
- **Boutique Work Experience:** Intern at local private wealth offices, boutique M&A firms, or early-stage VC funds to build early CV entries.
    
**Year 3: Summer Analyst Internship (Main Gateway)**
- **Application Timeline:** Applications for summer analyst roles open 10–12 months in advance.
    
- **Technical & Behavioral Prep:** Practice LBO mechanics, accounting questions, and STAR-format behavioral stories.
    
- **Return Offer:** Convert the summer internship into a full-time full-time analyst offer upon graduation.
    
**Year 4: Career Launch & Buy-Side Transition**
- **IB / WM Path:** Begin as a full-time analyst.
    
- **PE Pipeline:** Prepare for on-cycle/off-cycle PE recruiting during your first year in investment banking.
    
- **VC Path:** Join directly through undergraduate analyst programs, scout programs, or via 1–2 years in tech operations/banking.
    
## High-Impact No-Code Portfolio Projects
1. **M&A / Investment Deal Flow Tracker**
      
    - **Execution:** Maintain a public database analyzing recent M&A transactions or VC funding rounds.
        
    - **Value:** Demonstrates commercial awareness and deal logic without needing code.
        
2. **Paper Trading Portfolio & Thesis Log**
      
    - **Execution:** Track a virtual $100,000 portfolio with a written buy/sell thesis for every position.
        
    - **Value:** Shows risk management and asset allocation understanding for Wealth Management and Equity research.
3. **Local Business Financial Audit**
    - **Execution:** Help a local business or student organization analyze their cash flow and unit economics.
        
    - **Value:** Provides real-world advisory experience that sets your CV apart from peers
